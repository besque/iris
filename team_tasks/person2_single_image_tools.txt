ROLE: Single-Image Tools (VQA + one more: captioning or grounding)

These are the mandatory baseline tasks: VQA is required, plus either captioning or grounding. You own both.

RESEARCH FIRST (max 1 hour, then commit):
Find one open-weights remote-sensing VLM that can do VQA and captioning (and ideally grounding) in a single model, so you only load one model. Candidates to compare:
- GeoChat: https://github.com/mbzuai-oryx/GeoChat (VQA + grounding + captioning, weights on HuggingFace, most popular)
- LHRS-Bot: https://github.com/NJU-LHRS/LHRS-Bot
- SkyEyeGPT: https://github.com/ZhanYang-nwpu/SkyEyeGPT
Decide based on: can you actually get it running on our GPU/collab today, weights available, inference speed. Do not pick the "best" one, pick the one you can run by lunch.

WHAT TO BUILD:
1. backend/tools/vqa/: a class subclassing Tool from backend/tools/base.py. run() takes an image + question, returns ToolResult (text answer, confidence if the model gives one).
2. backend/tools/captioning/ OR backend/tools/grounding/: same pattern. If your model does grounding, prefer grounding (returns boxes in ToolResult.spatial), it demos better. Your call.
3. A tiny test script that runs your tool on 2-3 sample images so anyone can verify it works.

EVALUATION (if time allows, else hand to whoever is free):
Wire your tool into evaluation/eval_rsvqa.py against the RSVQA test split. Even scores on 100 samples are better than nothing.
